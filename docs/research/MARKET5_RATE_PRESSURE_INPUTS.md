# MARKET5 Research Input Set — Rate Pressure Context

## Corporate-bond leading factor

Add the following research inputs for NQ, Gold and Silver:

- US IG corporate-bond issuance
- US HY issuance
- IG/HY credit spreads
- new-issue concession when available
- large issuance calendar
- Treasury hedge / supply interaction
- US Treasury 2Y / 10Y / 30Y reaction
- real-yield reaction

Corporate issuance alone is **not** directional proof.

Required interpretation:

`CORPORATE SUPPLY + TREASURY SUPPLY + CREDIT SPREAD + TREASURY YIELD + REAL YIELD = RATE PRESSURE CONTEXT`

The factor is contextual and must be combined with existing market evidence before directional classification.
